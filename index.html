Example components

Live price ticker

A scrolling marquee of every team's current Stakeholder price and daily change, similar to a stock market ticker. The same strip can be repurposed for different use cases alongside other components shown here.

Matchup overlay

A transparent broadcast card showing live prices pre-game, then flipping to the final score once it ends — click it to step through a few past games.

Weekly newsletter

The rendered HTML email sent to traders each week, recapping the biggest movers and previewing upcoming matchups.
